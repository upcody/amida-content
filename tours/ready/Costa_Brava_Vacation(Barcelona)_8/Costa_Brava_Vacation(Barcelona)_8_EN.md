### Day 2

Breakfast.

Excursion in Barcelona. Barcelona - the capital of Catalonia, a magnificent European city located in the northeastern part of the Mediterranean coast of Spain, which is famous not only for its beautiful medieval center, but also for its extraordinary Art Nouveau buildings - creations of Antoni Gaudi and other eminent architects. Our combined city tour will begin with an external inspection of the "Sagrada Familia" temple. This is not just a temple, it is a real calling card of Barcelona. Among other buildings created by Antoni Gaudi, one can note the Casa Mila, which locals call La Pedrera ("The Quarry")

We will drive along Paseo de Gracia, the most aristocratic street in Barcelona and you will see the famous "Block of Discord" quarter, where the houses of modernist architects are located (Casa Mila (Pedrera), Casa Batllo, Casa Lleo Morera, etc.), Continuing our journey through Barcelona, we will find ourselves on the Paseo de Lluís Companys alley leading to the exhibition grounds in the Ciutadella Park, where the main attraction is the Arc de Triomphe, then we will go to the Christopher Columbus monument and to Plaza de España.

In your free time, we recommend taking a walk along Las Ramblas - the main pedestrian street of Barcelona and buying souvenirs as a keepsake.

Then we will go to the foot of Montjuic mountain, to the colorful performance of the Magic Fountain of Montjuic. The fountain has become a world-famous symbol of the city since 1992, when the Olympic Games were held in Barcelona. But even now, the amazing combination of colors, music and water choreography will not leave you indifferent.

Return to the hotel. Optionally we offer dinner at the hotel (surcharge). Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 3

Breakfast. Free day at sea. Optionally, we offer an "Author's tour of the Costa Brava coast" (surcharge 50&nbsp;€).

The beauty of this coast, with its emerald water bays immersed in greenery, is hard to overestimate. During the excursion we will visit the elite district of the city of Blanes - San Francisco (Saint Francis), located around the bay of the same name. The district was named after a small elegant church with an observation deck from which you can admire the sea landscapes.

You can also take a walk along the coast along the former 18th-century patrol path "Cami de Ronda"

Next we will go to the city of Lloret de Mar, where, optionally, you can visit the Clotilde Gardens (entrance ticket is paid additionally).

Then we will visit the city of Tossa de Mar, which is rightfully called the pearl of the Costa Brava. The city still serves as a source of inspiration for poets and artists to this day. We will walk through its ancient streets and enjoy breathtaking views opening from the height of the 11th-century fortress.

In your free time in Tossa de Mar you can swim in the emerald waters of the Costa Brava coast or take a sea trip on a small boat with a panoramic bottom through the grottoes and bays of the coast (ticket is paid additionally).

Return to the hotel. Optionally we offer dinner at the hotel (surcharge). Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 4

Breakfast.

Excursion to Girona and Figueres.

Girona is an ancient Spanish city with a rich history. Its medieval quarters still preserve the memory of events that took place here over many centuries and where during a walking tour through the quiet streets of the old city you will make a journey into the depths of centuries and hear entertaining legends about the inhabitants of ancient Girona. The Jewish quarter of Girona is considered one of the best preserved in Europe.

The city of Figueres is located in Catalonia and is the birthplace of Salvador Dali, the central square of the city bears the name of Gala and Dali. A visit to the Dali Theatre-Museum, designed by the artist himself (entrance to the museum is paid additionally) awaits you.

Return to the hotel. Optionally we offer dinner at the hotel (surcharge). Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 5

Breakfast. Rest at sea.

In the afternoon, excursion trip to Montserrat.

Montserrat is more than a thousand rocks that create a unique view and fascinate with their landscapes. The mountain range formed several million years ago and turned into bizarre fantastic figures created by nature. Thanks to the imagination of local residents, unusual peaks received names depending on what they resemble - "Elephant's Trunk", "Mummy", "Skull", "Bernard's Horse", "Guardian"... These places gave rise to many very different legends. One of them says that the bizarre shapes of the rocks are the creation of angels who descended from heaven to make the landscape of the mountain range more interesting and for the sake of this sawed the mountain peaks with golden files.

Montserrat translates as "sawed mountain". But the uniqueness of the mountain lies not only in the beauty of the unusual relief, but rather in the fact that the Benedictine Monastery of Montserrat (Monasterio de Montserrat) is located on it. This is an active monastery with an almost thousand-year history. And to this day it represents the spiritual symbol of Catalonia - monks live in it, performing daily divine services.

During the excursion you will learn the history of the monastery, hear many interesting legends about how the monastery arose and about the miracles associated with the statue of the Black Virgin. In your free time you can visit the basilica, bow to the main attraction of the monastery "Black Madonna", whom the Catalans affectionately call "La Moreneta". Around the monastery there are many picturesque hiking trails where you can walk and see ancient chapels and sketes, interesting religious sculptures and striking mountain landscapes.

Hiking around the monastery is the best opportunity to feel all the beauty of these places and enjoy the local unique landscapes.

Return to the hotel. Optionally we offer dinner at the hotel (surcharge). Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 6

Breakfast. Rest at sea.

Optionally, we offer an excursion trip to Perpignan and Collioure (surcharge 65&nbsp;€).

During this excursion you will visit the capital of the French department of Pyrenees-Orientales, the city of Perpignan and the town of Collioure, where you will enjoy the beauty of the Vermilion Coast of France.

First we will visit the city of Perpignan, where during an excursion through its historical center, you will see the medieval fortress-gate Castillet, enter the building of the Loge de Mer 14th century, the Cathedral. In your free time you can walk along the streets of the city, visit its numerous shops or drink coffee in cozy French cafes.

We will also visit the French town of Collioure, which is the pearl of the Vermilion Coast of France. Since time immemorial, a tradition has arisen in the city to paint houses in any color except white. Salmon, ocher, pink, olive houses combined with the color of the sky, the sea and the greenery of vineyards inspired the artist Henri Matisse and he created a new style of painting - "Fauvism". The city also houses significant architectural monuments – the majestic Royal Castle, which the Knights Templar began to build, the Church of Our Lady of the Angels.

Return to the hotel.

Optionally we offer dinner at the hotel (surcharge).
