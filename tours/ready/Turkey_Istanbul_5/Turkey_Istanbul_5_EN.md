### Day 1

Meeting of the group at Riga airport at 9:00. Flight from Riga at 11:00. First acquaintance with
Istanbul! After arrival and check-in, we will have an evening walk in the Sultanahmet district and a
visit to the famous Istiklal Street — the heart of modern Istanbul with its unique atmosphere,
shops, and historical tram.

### Day 2

Sightseeing tour of historical Istanbul. We will visit the majestic Topkapi Palace complex, which
served as the residence of Ottoman sultans for centuries. We will see the Hagia Sophia — a symbol of
the "Golden Age" of Byzantium, and the Blue Mosque with its unique architecture. We will walk
through the ancient Hippodrome and immerse ourselves in the atmosphere of the Grand Bazaar — one of
the largest covered markets in the world.

### Day 3

The Asian part of Istanbul and a Bosphorus cruise. An unforgettable Bosphorus cruise by boat awaits
us, where the city opens up from the water in all its beauty. We will visit the fragrant Spice
Market and the luxurious Dolmabahce Palace — the residence of the last sultans, built in Baroque and
Neoclassical styles. We will end the day at Camlica Hill in the Asian part, which offers the most
breathtaking panorama of all of Istanbul.

### Day 4

Yalova — one of the best wellness complexes in Turkey. New in the program: Excursion to the Yalova
thermal baths. You will visit the thermal complex with indoor and outdoor hot water pools, a hammam,
and a sauna. Healing springs with drinking and bathing water (40°C), rich in fluorine, sodium
chloride, and calcium, not only provide a relaxing effect but are also effective in treating many
diseases. A walk in the park after the procedures will allow you to enjoy the beautiful nature.

### Day 5

"See you soon, Istanbul...". Vacating rooms until 12:00. Free time for souvenir shopping or
independent walks. Transfer to the airport and flight home: Istanbul – Riga, at 16:00 – 19:05.
