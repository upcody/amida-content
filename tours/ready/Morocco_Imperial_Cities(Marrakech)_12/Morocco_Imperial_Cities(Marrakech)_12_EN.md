### Day 3

Breakfast.

City tour.

Marrakech is one of the most beautiful cities in the world.

Marrakech was one of the capitals of the kingdom during the reign of three dynasties of Moroccan sultans, it is here that a large number of museums and architectural monuments are concentrated, each of which is a masterpiece of art.

During the sightseeing tour, you will see the main sights and architectural monuments of the city, such as: the Koutoubia Mosque, the Bahia Palace (entrance tickets paid additionally ~10 €), a visit to the Ben Youssef Madrasa (entrance tickets paid additionally ~6 €) and many other monuments. We will visit the famous Jemaa el-Fnaa square. This is a real open-air theater. Who can you see here - yogis, snake charmers, healers, sorcerers, fruit and exotic spice traders.

Return to the hotel, rest.

### Day 4

Breakfast. Free day. Optionally, we offer an excursion trip to the amazing city of Essaouira (surcharge 65€).

Essaouira is an incredibly beautiful city on the coast of Morocco, which enthralls with its atmosphere, architecture and cultural heritage. This picturesque city on the Atlantic coast attracts tourists with its unique history, beautiful beaches and the hospitality of its inhabitants. The city amazes with its architecture - white houses with bright blue windows create a unique look that embodies the Moroccan style.

We will go for a walk through the historical center of Essaouira, which is listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and served as a popular filming location for Hollywood filmmakers. Episodes of such films and TV series as "Kingdom of Heaven" and "Game of Thrones" were created here, thanks to its unique atmosphere and architectural charm.

Here you will find narrow streets, shops with local handicrafts and authentic restaurants. Essaouira has a rich history. Founded as a free port by Sultan Mohammed ben Abdallah, the city played an important role in the trade of gold and ivory. At different times, the Portuguese and Berber tribes ruled here, which gave this city many cultural shades. The beaches of Essaouira are also known for their water sports, including surfing and windsurfing. Visiting Essaouira, you will discover the amazing world of Morocco and remain charmed by its beauty and hospitality forever.

Return to the hotel. Rest.

### Day 6

Breakfast. Check-out from rooms. Excursion trip Casablanca-Rabat-Meknes.

One of the main attractions of Casablanca is the largest mosque in Morocco - the Hassan II Mosque. The project of the French architect Michel Pinseau and the technologies of the late 20th century made it possible to create a mosque with a retractable roof capable of turning the prayer hall into an open terrace. Here is the tallest minaret in the world (about 200 m). The mosque is decorated with all the techniques that Moroccan masters possess: mosaics, stucco, painting and wood carving. The mosque building also houses a madrasa, a library and a national museum (entrance tickets paid additionally ~13 €).

You can judge the modern city by the lively Maarif quarter. This is one of the most prominent quarters of the city. Tourists love to visit these streets with elite shops and fashionable establishments, over which the Twin Center towers rise.

Transfer to Rabat.

Rabat - a fabulously beautiful city located on the shores of the Atlantic Ocean, will meet you with picturesque quarters full of blooming nature, a large number of architectural and historical monuments and the atmosphere of an ancient Maghreb state. During the sightseeing tour, you will see the Royal Palace, the Hassan Tower of the 12th century (symbol of Rabat), the Mohammed V Mausoleum, the historical Oudayas gate of the 12th century.

Next, a tour of Meknes.

Meknes is one of the three imperial cities of Morocco, which was able to preserve the atmosphere of that time more than others. Magnificent gardens are laid out inside the fortress wall of the old city, and El Hedim square is filled every evening with fortune tellers, jugglers, fire eaters and snake charmers.

During the sightseeing tour, we will see the city of Moulay Ismail of the 17th century, the stables, the mausoleum of Moulay Ismail, the historical Bab el-Mansour gate.

Departure to Fez. Accommodation in the hotel. Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 7

During the tour of Fez, we will see the sights of the Medina: the University of Al Quaraouiyine (IX-XII centuries), the Bou Inania Madrasa (entrance tickets paid additionally ~2 €), the Dar el Makhzen palace, as well as famous leather dressing workshops, ceramic factories, markets.

Return to the hotel. Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 8

Breakfast. Free day. Optionally, we offer an excursion trip to Chefchaouen (Blue City) (surcharge 60€).

Chefchaouen is a small city located on the slopes of the mountains between Tangier and Tetouan. The name of the city comes from the peculiar shape of the Rif mountain peak, resembling goat horns. Literally translated, "Chef Chaouen" means "look at the horns". Almost all houses in the old part of the city are painted in sky blue and white colors. The city is also known for marijuana producers with their large suburban plantations, as a result of which Chefchaouen received the name "capital of hashish".

Return to the hotel. Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 9

Breakfast. Check-out from rooms. Departure to Beni Mellal.

The road passes through the Middle Atlas Mountains. On the way, we will make a stop in the "Swiss city of Morocco" - Ifrane. You will have the opportunity to take many panoramic photos! Small landscape stops are also planned.

Arrival, city tour of Beni Mellal. The city is located at the foot of the Middle Atlas Mountains. During the tour we will visit the main attractions of the city and a park with a small cascade from the Ain Asserdoun spring.

Accommodation in the hotel. Overnight at the hotel.

### Day 10

Breakfast. Check-out from rooms. Excursion trip to Ouzoud Waterfalls.

An excursion trip to the most beautiful and largest waterfall in the country, the Ouzoud Waterfall, will allow you to get to know the nature of the Kingdom of Morocco better. Located in the foothills of the Atlas Mountains, it falls beautifully in a triple cascade from a height of 110 meters. The path to Ouzoud passes through olive trees. Towards evening, a surprise awaits tourists – many monkeys come to the watering hole at the waterfall. Tourists have accustomed the monkeys to treats that they bring with them. You will have the opportunity to examine the waterfall not only at its foot, but also from top to bottom.

Clay Berber houses combined with ochre-colored sandstone, appearing every now and then from behind the turns of the mountain serpentine, will allow you to take unforgettable photos and leave Morocco in your heart forever. At the end of the trip, we will give you the opportunity to have lunch in a real Berber restaurant (lunch is paid additionally ~15 euros).
