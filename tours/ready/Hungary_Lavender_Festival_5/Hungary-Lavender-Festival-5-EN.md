### Day 1

Early morning departure from Riga. Travel through Lithuania and Poland. Lunch break in Poland. Continuing through the Czech Republic. Arrival and overnight at a 3* hotel near Brno.

### Day 2

Breakfast. Travel to Hungary. Arrival in Budapest. Sightseeing tour of Budapest with a local guide (€20 if the package is not purchased). We will get acquainted with two historical parts: Budapest - Heroes' Square, Vajdahunyad Castle, State Opera House, Parliament building, St. Stephen's Basilica, Chain Bridge; and the Old Town of Buda - Matthias Church, Fisherman's Bastion, Trinity Column, Presidential Palace. Free time in the city for relaxation. Night at a 3* hotel in Budapest.

### Day 3

Breakfast. We offer the "Lavender Blossom" excursion (€35 if the package is not purchased). We head to Balaton, to the Tihany Peninsula - a paradise for growing lavender. Here you don't just smell the lavender; you hear it. Lavender is grown not only on large plantations but in every yard. Here you can find lavender perfume, soap, lavender beer, chocolate, and wine. Free time for relaxation and aromatherapy. Tihany Peninsula is a wonderful sacred place in Hungary, home to a Benedictine basilica with a magnificent panoramic view of the "Pannonian Sea" - Lake Balaton. In the evening, we offer a wine tasting of Balaton wines with national cuisine and Hungarian charm in the Budafok wine cellars. Night at a 3* hotel in Budapest.

### Day 4

Breakfast. Vacating rooms. We head to the magnificent Gödöllő Palace - the favorite summer residence of Empress Elisabeth (Sissi). This is a luxurious Baroque palace with halls that have survived many intrigues at the Viennese court. Audio guide excursion. At noon, transfer to Slovakia - a visit to the Corvinus thermal pools in Velky Meder. Hot springs created for rest, relaxation, and health. In the evening, departure to a hotel on the Polish-Czech border. Night at a 2* hotel.

### Day 5

Breakfast. Path through Poland and Lithuania. Late evening (night) arrival in Riga.
