Bergamo is a unique city with a rich history dating back centuries. Many famous composers, architects, commanders and artists were born here. Here you can simultaneously enjoy views of the Italian Alps and the Padan Plain. This is a city with Roman and Celtic roots, thanks to which historical monuments of the last 2500 years have been preserved here. For the last 600 years, successful silk manufacturers and merchants have chosen this city as their permanent residence, thanks to which many magnificent palaces, cathedrals and city squares have been erected in Bergamo.

### DAY 2

Breakfast. Check-out from rooms. Departure to Venice. Excursion in Venice (additionally paid boat 20 € and entry fee 15 €).

Venice - there is no such city on the whole planet. Built on piles, the city seems to float above the lagoon in transparent air filled with sunlight or appears through a fine mesh of rain like an engraving of an old master. Architectural creations, incredibly created by many generations on difficult terrain for development, in harmony with the water of canals and light form a unique harmony. This city can safely be called a city-museum. Here at every step there are masterpieces of architecture of past centuries, monuments and other sights. For example, the famous bridges: "Golden Bridge", "Bridge of Sighs" and "Bridge of Changers", the building of the Old and New Procuracies, the Venier-de-Leoni Palace, the Doge's Palace, the library, the Clock Tower, the Campanile bell tower with an observation deck, the grandiose Arsenal complex, the Cathedral of Santa Maria della Salute, the Frari Basilica and the Merceria shopping street. Most Venetian streets-canals are so narrow that you can easily rest your hands on the walls of opposite houses, while the buildings themselves reach a height of 7 floors.

Free time.

### DAY 3

Breakfast (for tourists going on an excursion, breakfast with them).

Optionally, we offer an excursion trip to Rome and the Vatican (surcharge 60 € + entry fee to Rome - 10 €).

Rome is the capital of Italy and one of the most visited cities in Europe. Rome is the main political, cultural, significant economic center of the country, one of the oldest and richest cities in the world in historical and cultural monuments.

The Vatican is a city-state located on the territory of Rome, its area is only 0.44 sq. km. The state consists of only one city, it was created by the Lateran Agreements between the Roman Catholic Church and the Italian state on February 11, 1929. The Vatican houses the residences of each of the existing popes, the interior and exterior of which deserves special attention and admiration. The Vatican is a real open-air museum city, where literally every meter has famous sights.

### DAY 4

Breakfast. Check-out from rooms.

Excursion program in Tuscany (Siena and San Gimignano) (entry fee to Siena – 5 €, entry fee to San Gimignano – 5 €).

Tuscany is not just one of the most famous corners of our planet, but also a concentration of beauty, UNESCO cities, the best wine in Italy and masters of landscape design.

In Siena, you can easily get lost, both in time and space — the main appearance and traditions of this city have not changed over the last 700 years. Its streets, as before, are divided into opposing contrade, forgetting about modernity. Siena easily transports us to the commune city of the XIV century, famous for its monuments: the cathedral, on which the best masters of the XIII century worked, and the refined Del Campo square — an incomparable example of medieval architecture.

San Gimignano has long become one of the most cozy and favorite corners of Italy for visitors. Millions of guests from all over the world flock here every year for the sake of colorful streets, Tuscan ceramics, mesmerizing views of the hills and for the sake of towers, thanks to which the name "Medieval Manhattan" has long been entrenched for this place. An equally important reason to get to San Gimignano is the opportunity to try ice cream in the best gelateria in Italy, which has become a world champion and prize-winner many times.

Overnight at the hotel.

### DAY 5

Breakfast. Excursion trip to Florence (~5 € public transport costs)

Florence is a city where the Renaissance flourished in the XV century with its main characters Donatello, Botticelli, Michelangelo and Leonardo da Vinci. In addition, banking was already flourishing here, thanks to which the gold florin appeared - the first "single currency of Europe". In Florence, Dante, Boccaccio and Petrarch create the pillars of European literature. Here, on the banks of the Arno, Giotto and Arnolfo di Cambio created. And nowadays you can slowly walk along the Old Bridge and the Dante quarter, sit on the Signoria Square and in the open-air museum - Loggia dei Lanzi — all this in the scenery of the incomparable "workshop of Europe".

Overnight at the hotel.

### DAY 6

Breakfast.

Excursion trip to Pisa and Genoa (surcharge 50 € + entry fee to Pisa – 8 €).

It is difficult to find a person who would not be familiar with the image of the Leaning Tower of Pisa. This is more than just a monument — it is a symbol and memory of the great architects who created in the Republic of Pisa. It was to the Leaning Tower of Pisa in the XV century that the first excursion tours in Europe began to be organized. Over the centuries, its popularity is becoming more and more. But few people, visiting the famous Square of Miracles, pay attention to Pisa itself, to its beautiful streets pierced by arches-galleries, the university quarter where the great researcher Galileo Galilei worked.

Genoa - an ancient port city located on the shores of the Gulf of Genoa of the Ligurian Sea, is the owner of the largest historical center in Europe and is considered one of the most beautiful cities in Italy. In addition, Genoa is a center of heavy industry and shipbuilding. The main attraction of the Genoese port is the lighthouse, known as "la Lanterna". The city has many historical monuments, such as the Doge's Palace, the gates of medieval Genoa - Porta Soprana, the Cathedral of San Lorenzo, the house of Christopher Columbus, the central square Piazza De Ferrari.

Free time.

### DAY 7

Breakfast. Check-out from rooms. Excursion program "Verona and Lake Garda" (entry fee to Verona – 5 €).

Verona is a city of immense charm and romanticism.

Arriving in Verona, it is impossible not to pay attention to the uniqueness and beauty of the streets and the abundance of the largest and brightest squares in Italy.

Here you can see places associated with Dante Alighieri, stroll through the streets remembering the heavy tread of the Scaliger family, and visit the courtyard of Juliet's house, where, under the famous balcony, in the place of young Romeo, several generations of lovers have been making wishes and bringing them to life. The city invites everyone to take a walk along the embankments of the Adige River sung by poets and get acquainted with its Roman past and monuments that have reminded us of the greatness of the Roman Empire for 2000 years. Verona is home to one of the few surviving amphitheaters — the Arena di Verona, a place of gladiator fights in the past and magnificent opera productions in the present.

Lake Garda is the largest lake in Italy. The lake is "shared" by three Italian regions – Lombardy (province of Brescia), Veneto (province of Verona) and Trentino – Alto Adige (province of Trento). The ancient Romans called the lake Benaco, and the name Garda was fixed for it already in the Middle Ages, it comes from the town of the same name in the province of Verona. According to some documents of the VIII century, the name Garda comes from the German warda, that is, "guard", "place of observation". The Veronese part of the Garda coast, with its mild climate, is also called the "Olive Riviera", and the coast belonging to Brescia is famous for its lemon gardens arranged in the form of terraces, for which it is called the "Lemon Riviera".

For many centuries, Sirmione – a city located on a peninsula that divides the southern part of Lake Garda in two - has been popular with travelers who have heard about its natural beauties and historical monuments. The city preserves important evidence of the Roman era and the Middle Ages, remembers the voices of artists and poets – such famous people of art as Catullus, Joyce, Tennyson, Maria Callas visited here.
